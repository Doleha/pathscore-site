Deep dive

AWS Solutions Architect Professional

AWS's associate-to-professional discount voucher is a concrete, useful detail that doesn't show up often in secondhand coverage: if you've already passed an AWS associate exam, you get $150 off this one, directly from AWS. Don't start studying without confirming whether you should be prepping for SAP-C02 or SAP-C03; we confirmed directly with AWS that SAP-C03 opened for registration October 27, 2026, right around this research date.

Azure Solutions Architect Expert

Azure's structure stands out: it's the only one of the three that explicitly requires holding a separate, lower-tier cert first. If you already have AZ-104 from an earlier associate-level path, the Expert credential is a direct continuation. If you don't, budget for both exams, not just AZ-305, since the $330 total in exam fees is a structural fact, not a price range you can shop around.

Google Professional Cloud Architect

Google Professional Cloud Architect has two named, citable surveys behind its salary range, stronger sourcing than most certs in this cluster: a Global Knowledge survey that named this credential a top-paying certification in a specific year, and a Skillsoft figure around $190,200 from 2024. It's worth citing those studies directly rather than through a blog that merely mentions them.

Who each one is for

AWS Solutions Architect Professional fits best for: anyone who already holds an AWS associate cert and wants the clearest, most immediate next step, helped along by the $150 discount voucher.

Azure Solutions Architect Expert fits best for: AZ-104 holders working in a Microsoft shop who want the direct continuation of that path, as long as they budget for both exams.

Google Professional Cloud Architect fits best for: candidates who want the strongest named salary sourcing of the three, even though GCP's market share trails AWS and Azure.

FAQ

Should I study for SAP-C02 or SAP-C03?

Confirm directly with AWS before you start. We confirmed on AWS's own certification page that SAP-C03 opened for registration October 27, 2026, right around this research date, so older SAP-C02-specific prep material may already be out of date.

Do I need AZ-104 before attempting Azure Solutions Architect Expert?

Yes. This is a structural requirement, not a suggestion: the Expert credential requires holding AZ-104 in addition to passing AZ-305, for $330 total in exam fees rather than the $165 cost of AZ-305 alone.
