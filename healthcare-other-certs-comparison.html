Deep dive

CPC (Certified Professional Coder)

CPC is a genuine non-clinical healthcare entry point, distinct from every other cert PathScore has covered: medical coding and billing, not patient care. We checked AAPC's own site directly: current pricing is $425 for one exam attempt or $499 for two, which supersedes the lower $399 and $300–$400 figures some third-party sources still cite. It requires 36 continuing education units every two years to maintain. AAPC's own 2025 survey is the strongest salary source here and is worth citing directly.

PMI-ACP (Agile Certified Practitioner)

PMI-ACP fills a real gap left by the software-specific Scrum certs (CSM, PSM I) covered in an earlier PathScore comparison: it's PMI's Agile credential for any industry, not just software teams. Its eligibility requirements (8 months of Agile-specific experience, 12 months of general PM experience, and 21 hours of Agile education) reflect that broader scope. Its salary data has one clear outlier that should be excluded rather than blended into an average.

CompTIA Cloud+ (CV0-004)

CompTIA Cloud+ is the vendor-neutral alternative to the AWS, Azure, and GCP associate-level cloud certs PathScore has already covered. We confirmed the current post-June-2026-hike pricing directly on CompTIA's own store: $399 for the standard voucher and $519 for the bundle with retake assurance. Its role-split salary figures ($65K–$95K admin vs. $95K–$160K engineer) are more informative than the flat average, since they show the real range by seniority.

Pros & cons

CPC: Pros

  • Genuine non-clinical healthcare entry point
  • Strongest employer-specific salary sourcing of the three (AAPC's own survey)

CPC: Cons

  • Requires separate paid AAPC membership for member-rate pricing

PMI-ACP: Pros

  • Only major Agile cert built for any industry, not just software

PMI-ACP: Cons

  • Combined experience eligibility requirements are more involved than most certs here
  • No PayScale-specific salary page exists as a fallback source

CompTIA Cloud+: Pros

  • Vendor-neutral, no need to commit to AWS, Azure, or GCP first
  • Clear role-split salary data by seniority

CompTIA Cloud+: Cons

  • Price just rose under CompTIA's June 2026 across-the-board hike

Who each one is for

CPC fits best for: candidates wanting a non-clinical way into healthcare, with the strongest employer-specific salary sourcing of the three.

PMI-ACP fits best for: professionals working Agile in a non-software industry (operations, marketing, manufacturing) who want a credential built for that, assuming they meet its combined experience requirements.

CompTIA Cloud+ fits best for: anyone who wants a vendor-neutral cloud credential without committing to AWS, Azure, or GCP specifically.
