Quick verdict

Tightest budget and want the cheapest way to prove you know the basics? Take ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC) at $199. Need a credential that satisfies DoD 8570/8140 for a federal or contractor role, or want the more broadly recognized entry-level cert? Take CompTIA Security+, currently $439 list on version V7 (exam code SY0-701). The two are not mutually exclusive: many readers take CC first as a cheaper on-ramp, then Security+ once they are ready for the bigger exam fee.

Heads up: Security+ is mid-transition. V8 is expected to launch on or around November 17, 2026, with expanded coverage of AI-related risks, security operations, and modern environments. The current V7 (SY0-701) keeps testing through June 11, 2027 for English candidates. For the full version-transition detail, see our CompTIA Security+ vs. CEH vs. CISSP comparison.

Deep dive

ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC)

CC is a $199 exam-only credential (Regular option) with no work-experience requirement. It is built to the ISO/IEC 17024 personnel-certification standard, is ANAB accredited, and is approved by the Department of Defense. The Exam Only option gives you a 365-day window and 1 attempt. ISC2 also sells an Exam Only with Peace of Mind Protection option, which includes two attempts within 180 days; check its current price on ISC2's site before you buy.

ISC2's free One Million Certified in Cybersecurity program is background, not an offer. ISC2 closed new enrollments in it effective May 20, 2026. Holders of an unexpired exam code can still schedule and sit the exam through December 31, 2026.

CompTIA Security+

Security+ remains the more broadly recognized of the two at the entry level, mainly because of its DoD 8570/8140 mandate across many federal and contractor security roles. At $439 list (resellers around $365 to $395), it costs more than double CC's $199 exam fee. It is also mid-transition: V8 is expected to launch on or around November 17, 2026, while the current V7 (SY0-701) keeps testing through June 11, 2027 for English candidates. For the full detail on that transition, including retirement dates for other languages, see our CompTIA Security+ vs. CEH vs. CISSP comparison.

Pros & cons

ISC2 CC: Pros

  • Lowest price of the two at $199
  • No work experience required
  • Built to the ISO/IEC 17024 standard and approved by the Department of Defense

ISC2 CC: Cons

  • Newer, less broadly recognized credential than Security+
  • Does not carry Security+'s DoD 8570/8140 mandate for federal and contractor roles
  • The free One Million program has closed to new enrollments

CompTIA Security+: Pros

  • DoD 8570/8140-mandated for many federal and contractor roles
  • More broadly recognized name among employers and training providers
  • No prerequisites, despite the stronger recognition

CompTIA Security+: Cons

  • More than double CC's price at $439 list
  • Version change in progress: V7 testing ends June 11, 2027 for English candidates, while V8 rolls out
  • Readers starting prep now should confirm which version their study material covers

Who each one is for

ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC) fits best for: readers on a tight budget who want the cheapest credible way to prove baseline cybersecurity knowledge, with no work experience and no federal compliance mandate to satisfy.

CompTIA Security+ fits best for: readers who need a credential that satisfies DoD 8570/8140 for a federal or contractor role, or who want the more broadly recognized entry-level security certification even at a higher price.

FAQ

Is ISC2 CC a substitute for Security+ on a resume?

Not for roles that require Security+ by name, such as most that fall under the DoD 8570/8140 mandate. CC is a legitimate, lower-cost entry-level credential, but it does not carry that same compliance mandate.

Can I still get ISC2 CC for free?

No. ISC2 closed new enrollments in its free One Million Certified in Cybersecurity program effective May 20, 2026. If you already hold an unexpired exam code, you can still schedule and sit the exam through December 31, 2026. Otherwise, the Exam Only option is $199.

Should I take CC before Security+?

If budget is the main constraint, CC at $199 is a reasonable lower-cost way to validate baseline knowledge first. It is not a prerequisite for Security+, so going straight to Security+ is also a valid path if you do not need the cheaper on-ramp.

Is Security+ V7 still worth taking right now?

Yes, if you can finish your prep and sit the exam before its retirement window closes (June 11, 2027 for English candidates). For the full version-transition detail, see our CompTIA Security+ vs. CEH vs. CISSP comparison.
