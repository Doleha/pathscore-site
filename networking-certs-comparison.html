Quick verdict

New to networking? Start with Network+: it's vendor-neutral, cheaper, and the material transfers regardless of which vendor's gear you end up working on. Working toward a Cisco-specific role? CCNA is worth the jump, reflected in both its higher Payscale average and its alignment with the higher-paying Computer Network Architect occupation track.

Deep dive

CompTIA Network+ (N10-009)

Vendor-neutral networking fundamentals, and the right first networking cert for almost everyone. Payscale reports $83K avg base (9,359 responses); the matching BLS role, Network and Computer Systems Administrators, reports a higher median of $99,130/yr (May 2025 OOH), since the occupation figure includes senior respondents the cert-holder survey doesn't fully capture.

A note on pricing: we could not independently confirm a standalone exam-only voucher price for N10-009 on CompTIA's own site at the time of writing. Confirmed bundle prices there are Practice+Exam $548, Learn+Exam $916, and Complete $978. If you've seen $399 quoted elsewhere, treat it as unverified until CompTIA's site shows it directly.

Cisco CCNA (200-301)

Worth the jump if you're targeting a Cisco-specific role or your employer runs Cisco infrastructure. Payscale reports $95K avg base (5,933 responses); the senior CCNA/design lane, BLS Computer Network Architects, reports $130,390/yr median (May 2024 OOH). We could not find a credible source for CCNA's typical study hours or job-posting-volume figures; the "$15K bump, breakeven in 4 months" claims recurring across SEO sites aren't traceable to a real source.

Pros & cons

CompTIA Network+ (N10-009): Pros

  • Vendor-neutral: knowledge transfers regardless of employer's gear
  • Cheaper and faster than CCNA

CompTIA Network+ (N10-009): Cons

  • Lower Payscale average than CCNA ($83K vs. $95K)
  • Less differentiated for Cisco-specific roles

Cisco CCNA (200-301): Pros

  • Higher Payscale average than Network+ ($95K vs. $83K)
  • Aligns with the higher-paying Computer Network Architect occupation track

Cisco CCNA (200-301): Cons

  • No retake discount, plus a mandatory 5-day wait after a fail
  • Cisco doesn't publish official study-hour guidance

Who each one is for

CompTIA Network+ (N10-009) fits best for: anyone new to networking, regardless of eventual vendor specialization.

Cisco CCNA (200-301) fits best for: anyone working in or targeting a Cisco-heavy network environment.

FAQ

Should I get Network+ before CCNA?

Yes, if you're tight on time or budget. Network+ knowledge reduces CCNA study time meaningfully, so sequencing them beats skipping straight to CCNA cold; they aren't really competitors, they're sequential.

Is the "CCNA pays $15K more" claim true?

We checked, and that specific figure isn't traceable to a real source. Several "breakeven in X months" and "$15K salary bump" claims about CCNA recur across multiple SEO sites in near-identical phrasing, which is a strong signal they're templated, not researched. Trust the sourced Payscale and BLS figures in the table above instead.
