Deep dive

PMP (Project Management Professional)

PMI runs both PMP and CAPM, and they aren't really competing for the same candidate. PMP remains the strongest single cert in this cluster by reported salary, and PMI's own 2024 salary survey is a real, named, primary source worth pulling directly for exact figures rather than taking it secondhand. The fee did increase in 2026: it now stands at $425 for PMI members and $675 for nonmembers, confirmed directly on pmi.org.

CAPM (Certified Associate in Project Management)

CAPM changed meaningfully in August 2024: PMI dropped the old experience-based eligibility path, so it now requires a secondary degree plus the 23-hour contact course, with no "years of experience instead" option. That's a real structural fact worth knowing before you plan around older eligibility rules you might find in outdated guides. It's consistently described as well below PMP's median, even though the exact comparison figure varies by source.

Six Sigma Green Belt & Black Belt

"Six Sigma certification" isn't one credential. ASQ, IASSC, and the Council for Six Sigma Certification all issue their own Green Belt and Black Belt credentials, at different price points and with different market recognition. We've used ASQ's own published fees above since ASQ is one of the more established issuing bodies, but confirm which body your target employer actually expects before you pay for training. Black Belt is worth it once you're leading improvement projects rather than just contributing to them.

PRINCE2 Foundation + Practitioner

PRINCE2 carries the strongest UK and European signal of any cert PathScore has researched so far. If your target market or employer is UK-based, the UK-specific salary range is more relevant than the general US-style averages quoted elsewhere for this cert. Its US recognition is real but less dominant than PMP's.

Pros & cons

PMP: Pros

  • Strongest reported salary premium in this cluster, backed by PMI's own named survey
  • Broadest hiring-manager recognition of the four

PMP: Cons

  • Fee rose in 2026 to $425 member / $675 nonmember, a meaningful jump from prior pricing
  • Requires verified work experience to qualify

CAPM: Pros

  • Realistic entry point into PMI's credential ecosystem without years of work history
  • Lower cost and shorter study time than PMP

CAPM: Cons

  • Salary data sits well below PMP's median
  • Since 2024, requires a degree plus the 23-hour course, no experience-based path

Six Sigma Green Belt / Black Belt: Pros

  • ASQ's published fees are the most reliable pricing in this cluster
  • Black Belt salary data ($95,000–$136,000) is competitive with PMP

Six Sigma Green Belt / Black Belt: Cons

  • Three different certifying bodies issue conflicting "Six Sigma" credentials
  • Black Belt study time isn't clearly reported anywhere

PRINCE2: Pros

  • Strongest regional fit for UK, European, and public-sector roles of any cert PathScore has covered
  • Flexible Foundation + Practitioner structure

PRINCE2: Cons

  • UK and US pricing estimates conflict significantly; confirm against PeopleCert's regional list
  • Less dominant than PMP outside UK/Europe

Who each one is for

PMP fits best for: project managers who already qualify by experience and want the strongest single credential and salary signal in this cluster.

CAPM fits best for: early-career candidates or those who don't yet qualify for PMP's experience requirements.

Six Sigma Green Belt fits best for: professionals in process improvement, manufacturing, or operations roles at a lower cost and commitment than Black Belt.

Six Sigma Black Belt fits best for: those already leading improvement projects, not just contributing to them.

PRINCE2 fits best for: candidates targeting UK, European, or public-sector/government project management roles.

FAQ

What is PMP's current exam fee?

Confirmed directly on pmi.org as of November 2026: $425 for PMI members, $675 for nonmembers. If you've seen older figures around $405 to $555, those predate the increase.

Can I skip PMP and go straight for CAPM if I'm early in my career?

Yes. CAPM no longer offers an experience-based eligibility path (that changed in August 2024), so it now runs on a secondary degree plus the mandatory 23-hour course, making it the realistic entry point regardless of work history.

Which Six Sigma certifying body should I choose?

ASQ, IASSC, and the Council for Six Sigma Certification all issue their own versions at different price points and recognition levels. Confirm which one your target employer or job posting actually expects before paying for training.
