Deep dive

Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate

Has multiple disagreeing salary figures and a specific, citable outcome stat worth pulling straight from Google or Coursera's own program page rather than repeating from a secondhand blog. Don't assume the subscription pricing is fixed; sources disagree on exact current terms, so confirm before you pay.

Azure Data Fundamentals (DP-900)

Honest about being a role-baseline salary figure, not a cert-specific one. It's also structurally unusual: it never expires, but requires a free annual online renewal assessment to stay current, a lighter-touch model than either a hard expiration or no renewal at all.

AWS Machine Learning Engineer Associate

The most honestly framed salary data of the group: both cited numbers come with the explicit caveat that the certification itself doesn't produce that pay, it's associated with a role that already pays well. Mind the version transition: MLA-C01 retires September 28, 2026 and is replaced by MLA-C02, confirmed directly against AWS's own certification page.

IBM Data Science Professional Certificate

Has essentially no usable salary data at all. We're not filling that gap with an estimate; if you're choosing this cert, choose it for the curriculum, not for a promised pay outcome, since the research turned up nothing credible to promise.

Who each one is for

Google Data Analytics fits best for: newcomers to data who want the broadest, most affordable entry point with the most (if inconsistent) salary data.

Azure Data Fundamentals fits best for: IT professionals already in a Microsoft shop who want a quick, low-stakes data credential.

AWS ML Engineer Associate fits best for: candidates targeting machine learning engineering roles specifically, who confirm the MLA-C01-to-C02 version transition before studying.

IBM Data Science fits best for: learners who want deep technical breadth and don't need a salary promise to justify the time.

FAQ

Is the AWS ML Engineer Associate exam changing?

Yes. We confirmed directly on AWS's own certification page that MLA-C01, priced at $150, remains current through September 28, 2026, when it's replaced by MLA-C02 at beta pricing of $75. Confirm which version you're prepping for if you're starting after that date.

Do any of these certs come with a verified salary boost?

Only AWS ML Engineer Associate's sources explicitly separate the role's pay from the cert's own lift, which is the most honest framing in this group. Google Data Analytics has multiple disagreeing figures, Azure DP-900's numbers describe the role rather than the cert, and IBM Data Science has no reliable cert-specific data at all.
