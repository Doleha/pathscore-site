How we score a path

Every comparison runs through the same four checks, so scores mean the same thing article to article.

1

Cost & value

Sticker price weighed against what you actually get: certificates, projects, mentorship, job support.

2

Outcomes

Completion rates, recognized credentials, and whether employers or industries actually recognize the program.

3

Time & format

Self-paced vs. cohort, expected hours per week, and realistic time to finish.

4

Support

Instructor access, community, and how good the help actually is when you're stuck.
