SHRM-CP/SCP vs. HRCI PHR/SPHR: Which HR Certification Is Worth It? (2026)

By The PathScore Team Updated November 9, 2026 6 min read

Two bodies, two philosophies: SHRM-CP/SCP tests HR competencies through SHRM's own framework, while HRCI's PHR/SPHR line tests a longer-established, knowledge-based body of HR law and practice, and the two price structures work differently enough that cost alone can decide which one fits your budget.

Deep dive

SHRM-CP / SHRM-SCP

SHRM prices its CP and SCP exams on a sliding scale by timing and membership rather than one flat fee. Early-bird registration runs $350 for members and $450 for nonmembers; once the early-bird window closes, standard pricing rises to $399 for members and $499 for nonmembers. Both tiers include nonrefundable fees baked into the price: $50 for early bird, $49 for standard. SHRM also offers discounted rates for military members and students, with the lowest published rate being $150 for early-bird student members, a substantially cheaper entry point than any HRCI tier below PHR.

HRCI aPHR / PHR / SPHR

HRCI structures its pricing differently: every credential carries a flat $100 nonrefundable application fee, then a separate exam fee that scales with the credential's seniority. aPHR, the only entry-level credential in this set with no HR experience required, costs $300 in exam fees for an all-in total of $400. PHR and its international counterpart PHRi cost $395 in exam fees for an all-in total of $495. SPHR, SPHRi, and GPHR sit at the top of HRCI's line, each at $495 in exam fees for an all-in total of $595. Because HRCI's fee structure is flat and does not depend on membership or registration timing the way SHRM's does, it is more predictable to budget for, even though it is not necessarily cheaper.

Pros & cons

SHRM-CP / SHRM-SCP: Pros

  • Lowest possible entry cost in this comparison: as low as $150 for early-bird student members
  • Early-bird pricing rewards planning ahead; SHRM membership can lower the price further

SHRM-CP / SHRM-SCP: Cons

  • Pricing depends on timing and membership status, so the final cost is less predictable than HRCI's flat structure
  • Nonmember standard pricing, at $499, is the highest nonmember rate in this comparison

HRCI aPHR / PHR / SPHR: Pros

  • Flat $100 application fee applies to every tier, making the total predictable regardless of timing
  • aPHR is purpose-built for candidates with no HR experience yet, which SHRM's line does not directly offer

HRCI aPHR / PHR / SPHR: Cons

  • No published early-bird discount in this research, so there is no way to beat the price by registering early the way SHRM allows
  • SPHR/SPHRi/GPHR's $595 all-in total is the highest price in this comparison

Who each one is for

SHRM-CP/SCP fits best for: practicing HR professionals who can plan ahead for early-bird pricing or qualify for a military or student discount, and who want the lowest possible entry cost.

HRCI aPHR fits best for: people with no HR experience yet who want a credential built specifically for entry-level candidates, something SHRM's CP/SCP line does not directly address.

HRCI PHR/SPHR fits best for: HR professionals who want one predictable, flat total with no membership or timing decisions, and who are comparing a mid-level (PHR) or senior/strategic (SPHR) role requirement.

FAQ

Is SHRM-CP or HRCI PHR cheaper?

It depends on timing and membership. SHRM-CP's early-bird member rate ($350) is cheaper than PHR's all-in total ($495), but SHRM's standard nonmember rate ($499) is more expensive than PHR. There is no single cheaper option across all cases.

Which credential should I get with no HR experience?

HRCI's aPHR is the only credential in this comparison built specifically for entry-level candidates with no HR experience required, at a $400 all-in total.

Why does SHRM pricing have so many tiers?

SHRM prices by registration timing (early bird versus standard) and membership status (member versus nonmember), plus discounted rates for military and student candidates. HRCI instead uses one flat application fee plus a fixed exam fee per credential, so its price does not change with timing.

Are there student or military discounts for HRCI credentials?

This research did not confirm HRCI discount rates; we do not assert one exists. Check HRCI's own exam-fees page before relying on either answer.
