Deep dive

CompTIA A+

The 20+ year default answer in job postings that name a credential at all for help-desk roles, including government and contractor positions. Payscale reports $78K avg base (12,167 responses); the closest BLS match, Computer Support Specialists, reports a lower median of $61,860/yr (May 2025 OOH), likely because Payscale's cert-holder survey includes people who've moved beyond pure help-desk roles since certifying. Present both figures, not just the higher one.

Google IT Support Professional Certificate

Best for candidates who want a guided curriculum and a direct pipeline: Google's named consortium of 150+ hiring partners commits to considering certificate holders. Coursera's official program page states 75% of US graduates report a positive career outcome within 6 months (2022 US graduate survey); note this corrects an outdated 82% figure that traces to a 2020-era news article and has since been superseded. The page also cites a $70,000 median entry-level IT support salary (Lightcast job-posting data), which is a role/market figure, not a per-certificate-holder claim.

FAQ

Is the 82% positive outcome stat for the Google IT cert accurate?

No, that figure is outdated. Coursera's current official program page states 75%, footnoted to a 2022 US graduate survey. The 82% number traces to a 2020-era news article and has been superseded; we're correcting it here rather than repeating a stale claim as current fact.

Should I get both A+ and the Google IT Support Certificate?

Not unreasonable: they're not mutually exclusive, and some help-desk job postings name either/both as acceptable. If budget-constrained, the Google cert is cheaper upfront if you move through it in 3–4 months, but becomes more expensive than A+ if you take the full 6+ months at $49/mo.
